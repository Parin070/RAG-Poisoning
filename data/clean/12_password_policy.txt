Title: Acme Corp Password Policy

All Acme Corp passwords must meet the following requirements:
- Minimum 12 characters.
- At least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one digit, and one special character.
- Cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 90 days. You will receive a reminder 14 days before expiration.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all remote access, including VPN and webmail.
If you forget your Active Directory password, use the self-service reset at https://itportal.acmecorp.internal/password-reset or call IT Help Desk at extension 4357.
