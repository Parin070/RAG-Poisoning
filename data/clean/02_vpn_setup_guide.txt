Title: VPN Setup Guide for New Employees

To set up the Acme Corp VPN on your laptop:
1. Download the GlobalConnect VPN client from https://itportal.acmecorp.internal/downloads.
2. Install the client and restart your computer.
3. Open GlobalConnect and enter the server address: vpn.acmecorp.internal.
4. Log in with your Active Directory username and your VPN password.
5. Select "Acme-Corp-Full-Tunnel" as your connection profile.
If the connection fails, ensure you are not on a restricted network and that your VPN password has been activated by IT.
