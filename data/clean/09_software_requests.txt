Title: Requesting New Software

To request installation of software not in the standard catalog:
1. Go to the IT Self-Service Portal and click "Software Request".
2. Provide the software name, version, business justification, and your manager's name.
3. IT Security will review the request within 3 business days for compliance and licensing.
4. If approved, IT will push the installation to your device remotely.
Do not install software from external sources without IT approval. Unauthorized software may be removed without notice per the Acceptable Use Policy.
