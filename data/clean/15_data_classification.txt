Title: Data Classification Policy

Acme Corp classifies data into four levels:
- Public: Information approved for external sharing (marketing materials, press releases).
- Internal: Default classification for most business documents. Share only within Acme Corp.
- Confidential: Sensitive business data (financials, strategy documents, customer PII). Requires encryption in transit and at rest.
- Restricted: Highly sensitive data (trade secrets, security credentials, legal holds). Access on a strict need-to-know basis with CISO approval.
Always label documents with their classification level. Mishandling classified data is a policy violation. See the full Data Classification Guide on the Security Portal.
