Title: Remote Work Policy

Acme Corp supports hybrid work. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. When working remotely, you must:
- Use the Acme Corp VPN for all access to internal systems.
- Be available on Acme Chat during core hours (10 AM - 4 PM local time).
- Ensure your home workspace meets ergonomic and security standards (locked screen, private area for calls).
Remote work arrangements are reviewed quarterly. Fully remote positions require HR and VP approval. Equipment for home offices can be requested through IT.
