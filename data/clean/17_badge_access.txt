Title: Building Badge and Access Control

Your employee badge grants access to your assigned building and floors. Badge access is configured during onboarding. To request access to additional areas (labs, server rooms, executive floors), submit a request through the Security Portal with manager approval. Lost badges must be reported immediately to Physical Security at extension 9000. A temporary badge will be issued within 1 hour. Tailgating (following someone through a secured door without badging in) is a security violation.
