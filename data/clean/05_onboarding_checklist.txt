Title: New Employee Onboarding Checklist

Welcome to Acme Corp! Please complete the following within your first week:
1. Collect your laptop and badge from the IT desk (Building A, Room 101).
2. Set up your Active Directory account using the temporary credentials in your welcome email.
3. Complete mandatory security awareness training on the Learning Portal.
4. Set up the VPN (see the VPN Setup Guide document).
5. Join the #new-hires channel on Acme Chat.
6. Schedule a 1-on-1 with your manager.
7. Review the Employee Handbook on the HR Portal.
Your onboarding buddy will reach out on your first day to help you get settled.
