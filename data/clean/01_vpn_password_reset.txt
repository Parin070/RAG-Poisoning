Title: How to Reset Your VPN Password

If you need to reset your VPN password, follow these steps:
1. Go to the IT Self-Service Portal at https://itportal.acmecorp.internal.
2. Click "VPN & Remote Access" and then "Reset VPN Password".
3. Enter your employee ID and the email address on file.
4. You will receive a temporary password via your registered email within 5 minutes.
5. Log in with the temporary password and set a new one that meets Acme Corp's password policy (at least 12 characters, one uppercase, one number, one special character).
If you have trouble, contact the IT Help Desk at extension 4357 or email helpdesk@acmecorp.internal.
